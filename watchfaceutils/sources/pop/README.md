Art and fonts from Prince of Persia (1989, MS-DOS; Jordan Mechner / Brøderbund), ripped sprites from
The Spriters Resource (https://www.spriters-resource.com/ms_dos/princeofpersia/). The game's art
remains the copyright of its owners; it is used here as fan art, non-commercially.

`raw/` holds the originals, untouched; `processed/` holds what was derived from them by hand.
Everything else is generated by scripts into `myfonts/`, `mywatchfaces/`, `dfu_screen.h` and `docs/pics/`.

- `raw/glyphs/res1032.png` ... `res1131.png`: the in-game text font, one image per character
  (file number = ASCII code + 1000; 1127-1131 are extra symbols, unused). 7 px cap height.
  `glyphs_to_gfx.py` (see its usage) converts them into `myfonts/PrinceOfPersia<N>x7b.h`, whole-pixel
  scales of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 (cap heights 7, 14, 21, 28 and 42 px), and `PrinceOfPersiaDigits4x7b.h`
  (digits and colon only, to save flash). Only 1x, 2x and Digits4x are #included; a font costs flash
  only once `myutils.h` includes it (3x about 4 KB, 4x about 7 KB, 6x about 15 KB).
- `raw/title_art.png`: the 320x200 title screen background (`res51.png` in the rip). The `persia` face
  and the DFU screen crop it to 200 px wide at its native pixels and Floyd-Steinberg dither it; do
  not resample it.
- `raw/wordmark_generated.jpeg`: "GRAY TIMER" lettering in the style of the game's title logo. The
  logo only has the letters of its own name, so this was drawn by an image model (Google's,
  prompted by the user with the logo).
- `processed/wordmark.png`: that lettering reduced to its pixel grid (7.8 px blocks -> 248x58,
  thresholded, white on black). Used by `generate_dfu_screen.py` and `generate_readme_banner.py`.
