Floyd-Steinberg dithered (1-bit, at final on-screen size) conversions of the machine renders
from the Horizon Wiki (https://horizon.fandom.com, files `<Machine>-Render.png`), background
and ground shadow removed via the alpha channel. Sized exactly for their boxes in
`generate_geometric.py` (dither patterns must not be resampled).

Text is Horizon (https://www.fontspace.com/horizon-font-f26000, freeware, non-commercial) with digits
and punctuation from Orbitron (OFL), which it lacks; converted by `ttf_to_gfx.py` (see its usage).
