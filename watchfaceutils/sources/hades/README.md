Floyd-Steinberg dithered (1-bit, at final on-screen size) Hades item and keepsake art, made with
`dither_source.py` and sized exactly for their boxes in `generate_geometric.py` (dither patterns
must not be resampled). All use `--luma 0.5` (centaurheart `--luma 0.25`) so saturated reds and
purples keep their highlights.

- `nectar`, `ambrosia`: user-supplied cartoon renders of the Nectar and Ambrosia gifts, `--gamma 1.4`.
- The rest are from the Hades Wiki (https://hades.fandom.com), files `Darkness.png`, `Chthonic_Key.png`,
  `Titan_Blood.png`, `Diamond.png`, `Gemstone.png`, `Charon's_Obol.png`, `Centaur_Heart.png`,
  `Pom_of_Power.png`, `Daedalus_Hammer.png`, `Old_Spiked_Collar.png`, `Pierced_Butterfly.png`,
  `Owl_Pendant.png`, `Cosmic_Egg.png`, `Bone_Hourglass.png`, `Skull_Earring.png`,
  `Chthonic_Coin_Purse.png`, `Eternal_Rose.png`, `Lucky_Tooth.png`.

Text is Caesar Dressing (Open Window, SIL OFL 1.1, Google Fonts), converted with `ttf_to_gfx.py`.
