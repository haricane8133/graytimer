# The build story

Gray Timer V2 came together over three weeks in October and November 2026.
After the first watch's display gave up in September, this one started again
from a fresh set of parts. Here's how it went, one photo at a time.

For the parts list and wiring, see the [hardware guide](HARDWARE.md). For the
finished watch, see the [gallery](GALLERY.md).

## 24 October: the parts

<p align="center">
  <img src="./pics/build/01_parts.jpg" alt="Watch parts laid out on a grey table" width="300"/>
</p>

Everything on one table: the e-paper panel, the old case and strap, two LiPo
batteries, a coin cell, the RV-8803 clock board, a DESPI-C02 display adapter,
and the tiny Seeed Xiao nRF52840 that runs it all.
